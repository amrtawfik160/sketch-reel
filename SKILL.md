# Sketch reel

A 1080×1920, 60 fps reel that looks like someone drawing a story live on an Excalidraw board: about 60 s for a connected story, 40-55 s for a hero or problem story, about 100 s for a breakdown. It runs on the `explainer-reel` pipeline (voice, timing, captions, music, render, checks); this skill adds the story method, the theme, and the animation system. [`reference/look.png`](reference/look.png) shows the look. [`examples/valor/`](examples/valor/) is the reference reel (Valor, the AI engineer, Oct 2026); [`examples/jev/`](examples/jev/) is the earlier one (Jev, an AI decision model, with a shop-owner hero and a book-a-call ending). [`examples/quant_story/`](examples/quant_story/) is the user's favourite (Oct 2026): a 62 s connected story about AI quantization, with one character (Sam), one number card carried through every shot, and one board of six stations the camera travels across, tilting from the dark row to the light row and pulling back for the recap. [`examples/valor_breakdown/`](examples/valor_breakdown/) is the 102 s breakdown (how Valor works, one request followed through every stage), with light and dark boards mixed and the curious-friend voice.

Read `~/.claude/skills/explainer-reel/SKILL.md` first: its steps, settings, and checks apply unless a step below says otherwise. Then read, before the step that needs them:
- [`reference/story.md`](reference/story.md) before the script: the story arcs, the connected-story rules, the retention ladder, the writing rules that make a script sound natural (the `unslop` rules for a voice), and the voice.
- [`reference/art.md`](reference/art.md) and [`reference/motion.md`](reference/motion.md) before writing scenes: the look, the board, the characters, the animation system, and the glitches already paid for.

## Steps

1. **Research.** Read the primary source (repo files with `gh api` or a clone, the product site, docs), then hunt for the facts an everyday viewer finds wild, funny, or slightly scary: things the subject did, names it uses, mistakes it made, money, who uses it. When the user names a reference reel, download it (`uvx yt-dlp`), transcribe it, and match its style, not its script. Done when you hold 10+ ranked facts, each with a quote and source, and you know which are self-reported.

2. **Story and script** → `lines.py`, written with the `reel-script` skill plus [`reference/story.md`](reference/story.md). Formulas stay `""`: this style has no formula row; big handwritten headline words do that job. For a reel that explains how something works, use the connected story arc. Check the script against the connected-story rules and every line against the writing rules in [`reference/story.md`](reference/story.md), then run the `de-slop` pass on the script in a fresh-context subagent before showing it. Show the user 3 hooks (A-C with your pick) and the table: voice, visual, and the retention device on each beat. Done when the user approves.

3. **Project.** Make `<proj>/`, copy `assets/excal_theme.js` to `<proj>/excal_theme.js`, and copy `assets/rough.js` and `assets/fonts/*.woff2` to `<proj>/assets/`. Copy the settings block of the example that matches the arc (`examples/quant_story/lines.py` for a connected story, `examples/valor/lines.py` for a hero story, `examples/valor_breakdown/lines.py` for a breakdown) (MODEL, VOICE, INSTR shape, SPEED, gaps, `CAPTIONS = True`, `MUSIC = "beat"`, `PRELUDE`, `ASSETS`). Done when `make_reel.py` builds with the stub `function scene(ctx) { return {}; }` and the frame shows the dark canvas.

4. **Voice and timing**: explainer-reel steps 4-5, with the voice rules in [`reference/story.md`](reference/story.md). Re-voice any line whose transcript misreads a name (Jev as "Jeff", Vercel as "Bercel") until it reads right; a loop of up to 3 tries per line is normal. Done when every line transcribes word for word and the total fits the arc (about 60 s for a connected story, 40-55 s for a hero or problem story, about 100 s for a breakdown) at `SPEED` ≤ 1.15.

5. **Scenes** → `scenes.js` + `sfx.py`. Start from `examples/quant_story/` for a connected story (`build_scenes.sh` joins `src/head4.js`, the generated `caps_gen.js`, `src/toolkit.js` and `src/story4.js`; it adds the station board, the screen-space cast, the tilt and measured word times), `examples/valor_breakdown/scenes.js` for wiped light and dark sections (`THEME_SW`, `CAPFIX`, the wipe), or `examples/valor/scenes.js`: keep its toolkit block (guarded actors, `tw`/`op`, rough.js `mk`/`rr`/`rline`/`rpath`/`rell`/`arrow`, `drawIn`, `note`, `typeOn`/`typeLines`, `scribble`, `headline`, `splat`, the figure system, `win`/`laptop`, the camera queue, the boil clock) and replace the story code. Lay the beats out on one board ([`reference/art.md`](reference/art.md)), time every beat to a cue, and follow [`reference/motion.md`](reference/motion.md). Done when every beat in the approved table exists and fires on its word.

6. **Build, review, render, deliver**: explainer-reel steps 7-9, plus the checks in [`reference/motion.md`](reference/motion.md) (Review), including an independent subagent review of every render. The cover is the first frame that shows the hook's paradox complete; when that is after 3 s (a number still counting up), say which frame and why. Deliver to the outputs folder; upload to Drive only after the user approves it. Done when the check, the side check, the transcript, and a full sheet pass are clean.
