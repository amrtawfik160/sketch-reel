## Logos (not bundled)

`examples/iceberg/` draws the logos of OpenAI, Anthropic and Claude, DeepSeek, Nvidia, Google, Microsoft, xAI and Grok, Hugging Face, Twitter, Nature, and Tay's avatar. These are trademarks of their owners and are not included in this repository or covered by its license. `examples/iceberg/lines.py` lists where to fetch each file: most come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0 for the SVG data; the marks stay trademarks). Check each brand's usage rules before you publish a reel that shows them.
